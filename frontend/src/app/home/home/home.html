Welcome to FlipIt

Your go-to app for creating and managing flashcards to boost your learning!

Manage topics

You can create and manage topics for anything you want to learn. Each topic can have an unlimited amount of cards!

Quiz yourself

When you want to test your knowledge, you can quiz yourself either in practice mode or quiz mode. In quiz mode, your answers will be remembered and will influence the cards you see in future quizzes: The more you answer a card correctly, the less times you will see it.
